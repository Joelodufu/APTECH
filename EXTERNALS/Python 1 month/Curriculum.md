**beginner → advanced** :

* **Duration:** 4 weeks
* **Schedule:** 3 days/week
* **Time:** 2 hours/day
* **Total time:** 24 hours

This plan balances **concepts (≈45%)**, **hands-on coding (≈45%)**, and **review/projects (≈10%)**.

---

## 🗓️ Weekly Structure (Recommended)

Each 2-hour session:

* **30 min** – Concept explanation
* **60 min** – Guided coding + exercises
* **30 min** – Practice challenge / review

---
