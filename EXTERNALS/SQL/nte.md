

# 🖥 **Advanced Photoshop Curriculum**

---

# 🎓 Outcome

After completing this curriculum, you will be able to:

✔ Retouch photos at agency standard
✔ Do complex compositing and creative manipulation
✔ Create brand-level posters & marketing graphics
✔ Automate workflows and handle professional files
✔ Build a portfolio suitable for real clients



## **MODULE 1: Advanced Workspace & Workflow Optimization**

**Goal:** Become faster, organized, and more efficient.

### Topics:

* Customizing workspace and keyboard shortcuts
* Creating and using advanced workspaces for specific tasks
* Color settings, color profiles & bit depth (RGB vs CMYK, sRGB/AdobeRGB)
* Smart Objects workflow & non-destructive editing
* File formats for print vs web (TIFF/PSD/PDF/PNG/JPEG)

### Practical Exercises:

* Create a custom workspace for retouching/design
* Convert an editable design from RGB to CMYK for print

---

## **MODULE 2: Advanced Layer & Mask Mastery**

**Goal:** Perfect layer control for complex compositions.

### Topics:

* Advanced layer blending strategies & blend if
* Vector masks vs pixel masks
* Clipping masks & nested masks
* Adjustment layer stack management
* Selective editing with luminosity masks

### Practice Tasks:

* Replace dull skies using blend-if
* Complex multi-mask compositing challenge

---

## **MODULE 3: High-End Photo Retouching**

**Goal:** Professional retouching for beauty & product photography.

### Topics:

* Frequency Separation (manual & action-based)
* Dodge & Burn micro vs global adjustments
* Skin texture preservation
* Eye, teeth, hair enhancement
* Product cleanup and reflection creation
* Liquify for body/shape correction

### Practice:

* Full beauty retouch (portrait)
* Retouch a product shot for advertising

---

## **MODULE 4: Advanced Selections & Compositing**

**Goal:** Seamlessly merge multiple images into realistic scenes.

### Topics:

* Complex selections (hair, fur, transparency)
* Pen tool advanced techniques
* Edge refinement techniques (channels, calculations)
* Photorealistic shadow & light matching
* Perspective matching & vanishing point tools
* Atmospheric integration (fog, depth, color grade)

### Projects:

* Create a movie poster composite
* Make a surreal digital art scene

---

## **MODULE 5: Lighting, Color & Advanced Color Grading**

**Goal:** Master cinematic and brand-level color control.

### Topics:

* Curves & levels advanced manipulation
* Gradient maps for stylized mood
* LAB color mode adjustments
* LUTS creation & applying
* Matching color between images
* Selective color grading for scene mood

### Practice:

* Create 3 different moods from one photo (warm, cinematic, dramatic)

---

## **MODULE 6: Graphic Design & Creative Manipulation**

**Goal:** Create professional design layouts.

### Topics:

* Typography advanced use (kerning, leading, tracking)
* Text effects & layer styles (gold, neon, glass)
* Shape tools & vector workflow
* Logo reconstruction inside Photoshop
* Poster, banner, flyer composition & layout rules

### Projects:

* Create a brand poster for an event or product
* Design a magazine cover

---

## **MODULE 7: Motion Graphics & Animation in Photoshop**

**Goal:** Use Photoshop for GIFs & visual motion.

### Topics:

* Timeline panel advanced animation
* Animated text effects
* Cinemagraph creation
* Looping GIF export optimization

### Exercises:

* Create an animated banner ad
* Make a cinemagraph (moving water/hair etc.)

---

## **MODULE 8: 3D & Mockups (Optional but Powerful)**

**Goal:** Add realistic product display capabilities.

### Topics:

* Smart object-based mockups
* Displacement maps
* Lighting & shadows in mockups
* 3D text, simple extrusion

### Practice:

* Create product mockup from template & from scratch

---

## **MODULE 9: Automation for Speed & Productivity**

**Goal:** Work like a pro — faster, smarter.

### Topics:

* Batch processing & scripts
* Creating and using actions
* Droplets for automated workflows
* Generative AI integrations (Neural Filters/Remove tool/Firefly)

### Practice:

* Build an action to auto-retouch & auto-resize photos
* Remove background for 200 images using batch workflow

---

## **MODULE 10: Portfolio Development & Capstone Project**

**Goal:** Produce work that can attract clients.

### Students must complete:

1. **Beauty Retouch Project**
2. **Movie/Creative Art Composite**
3. **Brand Poster or Product Mockup Design**
4. **Animated GIF or Cinemagraph**

Final Presentation:

* Compile best works into a portfolio layout
* Export for web & print
