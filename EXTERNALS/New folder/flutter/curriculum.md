# FLUTTER FULL COURSE

## 🏗️ **1. Dart Language Fundamentals (Foundation)**

Before Flutter, you need a solid grasp of **Dart**. Learn:

* Variables & Data Types (`int`, `double`, `String`, `List`, `Map`)
* Functions & Arrow Functions
* Control Flow (`if`, `for`, `while`, `switch`)
* Classes & Objects (OOP basics: inheritance, encapsulation, abstraction, polymorphism)
* Null Safety (`?`, `!`, `??`)
* Asynchronous Programming (`Future`, `async/await`, `Stream`)

👉 *Why?* Flutter is written in Dart. If you don’t understand Dart, you’ll struggle with even simple widgets.

---

## 🎨 **2. Flutter Basics (Widgets & UI)**

Start with the **building blocks of UI**:

* `main()` function & `runApp()`
* Difference between **StatelessWidget** & **StatefulWidget**
* Basic widgets: `Container`, `Row`, `Column`, `Stack`, `ListView`, `GridView`
* Layout & positioning (`Expanded`, `Flexible`, `Align`, `SizedBox`, `Padding`, `Margin`)
* Styling (`Text`, `BoxDecoration`, `Colors`, `Icons`)

👉 *Why?* These are the “lego blocks” of every Flutter app.

---

## 🔄 **3. State Management (How apps change data)**

Learn how Flutter manages data in apps:

* `setState` (local state)
* `InheritedWidget` (context-based state)
* Provider (most common beginner-friendly package)
* Then later: Riverpod, Bloc, MobX, Redux (depending on your project needs)

👉 *Why?* Without state management, your app can’t handle user interaction properly.

---

## 📲 **4. Navigation & Routing**

Learn how to move between screens:

* `Navigator.push`, `Navigator.pop`
* Named routes
* Passing data between screens
* Nested navigation (tabs, bottom navigation bar)

👉 *Why?* Every real-world app has multiple pages/screens.

---

## 📡 **5. Networking & APIs**

Learn how to connect Flutter apps to the internet:

* `http` package for REST APIs
* JSON serialization/deserialization (`jsonDecode`, models)
* Handling errors & loading states
* Secure API calls (tokens, headers)

👉 *Why?* Most apps rely on backend APIs (login, fetching data, payments).

---

## 💾 **6. Data Persistence (Local Storage)**

Store user data on the device:

* `SharedPreferences` (key-value storage)
* `sqflite` (SQLite database)
* `hive` (NoSQL key-value database)
* `moor`/`drift` (ORM-style database)

👉 *Why?* Apps need to remember user data even after closing.

---

## 🎨 **7. Advanced UI & Animations**

Enhance your app’s design:

* Implicit animations (`AnimatedContainer`, `AnimatedOpacity`)
* Explicit animations (`AnimationController`, `Tween`, `CurvedAnimation`)
* Hero animations (screen transitions)
* Custom widgets & reusable components

👉 *Why?* This makes your app feel professional & smooth.

---

## 🛠️ **8. Architecture & Best Practices**

Learn how to structure your code:

* MVC, MVVM, and **Clean Architecture**
* Dependency Injection (`get_it`)
* Separation of concerns (UI, business logic, data layers)
* Modularization for larger apps

👉 *Why?* Without this, big apps become messy and hard to maintain.

---

## 🚀 **9. Deployment & DevOps**

Prepare your app for the real world:

* Building APKs & IPAs
* Publishing to Play Store & App Store
* CI/CD basics (GitHub Actions, Codemagic, Bitrise)
* App signing, versioning, and release management

👉 *Why?* This is the final step — getting your app into users’ hands.

---

## 🧑‍💻 Suggested Learning Path (Step-by-Step Order)

1. **Dart Fundamentals**
2. **Basic Widgets & Layouts**
3. **Stateful vs Stateless Widgets**
4. **State Management (setState → Provider)**
5. **Navigation & Routing**
6. **Networking (APIs, JSON)**
7. **Local Storage**
8. **Advanced UI & Animations**
9. **Architecture & Clean Code**
10. **Deployment & CI/CD**
