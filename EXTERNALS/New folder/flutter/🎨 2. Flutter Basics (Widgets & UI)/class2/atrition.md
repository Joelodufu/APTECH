

### **Step 1: Generate 6 Hypothetical Questions (for the dashboard)**

These questions should be **data-driven** and answerable with pivot charts in Excel:

1. **Attrition by Department:**
   Which department has the highest employee attrition rate?

2. **Attrition by Age Group:**
   What age group experiences the highest attrition rate?

3. **Attrition vs Job Satisfaction:**
   Is there a relationship between job satisfaction and employee attrition?

4. **Attrition by Gender:**
   Does gender influence the likelihood of attrition?

5. **Attrition by Business Travel Frequency:**
   Does frequent business travel correlate with higher attrition?

6. **Attrition by Years at Company:**
   How does attrition vary with the number of years an employee has been with the company?

---

### **Step 2: Create Pivot Charts (Dashboard Sheet)**

In Excel:

* Insert Pivot Tables for each question.
* Add corresponding Pivot Charts (bar charts, pie charts, etc.).
* Place all charts on a separate **Dashboard Sheet** labeled “HR Dashboard”.

Example chart types:

* Q1: Column chart (Department vs Attrition %)
* Q2: Bar chart (Age Band vs Attrition)
* Q3: Scatter or column chart (Job Satisfaction vs Attrition)
* Q4: Pie chart (Gender % within Attrition)
* Q5: Column chart (Travel Frequency vs Attrition)
* Q6: Line or column chart (Years at Company vs Attrition Rate)

---

### **Step 3: Summary of the Six Questions**

After building the dashboard, write a short summary such as:

> **Summary:**
> The HR dashboard reveals that the **Sales department** experiences the highest attrition, especially among employees aged **25–34**. Attrition tends to be higher among employees with **low job satisfaction** and those who **travel frequently**. Gender differences are minimal, but attrition decreases as employees spend more years in the company.

---

### **Step 4: Choose a 7th Question for Hypothesis Testing**

For hypothesis testing, choose one deeper analytical question, for example:

**Question 7:**
*Is there a significant relationship between Job Satisfaction and Attrition?*

---

### **Step 5: Formulate the Hypothesis**

* **Null Hypothesis (H₀):** Job satisfaction has no significant relationship with employee attrition.
* **Alternative Hypothesis (H₁):** Job satisfaction significantly affects employee attrition.

We’ll then perform a **Chi-square test of independence** to test this relationship.
