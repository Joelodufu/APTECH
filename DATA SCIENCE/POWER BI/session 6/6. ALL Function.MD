# **Module 6: The ALL Function**

## **6.1 Concept**

The `ALL` function **removes filters** from a table or column, allowing calculations **ignoring current context**.
It’s commonly used for:

- Calculating **percent of total**
- Creating **rankings**
- Comparing a subset to the full dataset

**Syntax:**

```DAX
ALL(tableOrColumn)
```

---

## **6.2 Practical Examples**

**Example 1: Percent of Total Sales**

```DAX
% of Total Sales :=
DIVIDE(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    CALCULATE(SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]), ALL(Sales))
)
```

🔹 Calculates each row’s share of overall sales, ignoring filters.

---

**Example 2: Percent of Category Sales**

```DAX
% of Category Sales :=
DIVIDE(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    CALCULATE(SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]), ALL(Sales[Product]))
)
```

🔹 Shows each product’s contribution to its category.

---

**Example 3: Rank Products by Sales**

```DAX
Product Sales Rank :=
RANKX(
    ALL(Sales[Product]),
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    ,
    DESC
)
```

🔹 Assigns rank to each product based on total sales, ignoring filters.

---

**Example 4: Category Rank by Profit**

```DAX
Category Profit Rank :=
RANKX(
    ALL(Sales[Category]),
    SUM(Sales[Profit]),
    ,
    DESC
)
```

🔹 Ranks categories by total profit.

## **6.3 Exercises (Hands-On)**

1. **Percent of Total Profit**

   - Measure: Each product’s profit as % of **all product profits**.

2. **Product Contribution within Category**

   - Measure: Each product’s `SalesAmount` as % of total for its **category**.

3. **Top Product by Sales Rank**

   - Measure: Rank all products by `SalesAmount` in descending order.

4. **Category Rank by Total Sales**

   - Measure: Rank categories by total `SalesAmount`.

5. **Ignore Category Filter**

   - Measure: Total Sales ignoring any **category slicers**.

## **6.5 Exercise Answers (For Trainers)**

1. **Percent of Total Profit**

```DAX
% Total Profit :=
DIVIDE(
    SUM(Sales[Profit]),
    CALCULATE(SUM(Sales[Profit]), ALL(Sales))
)
```

- Shows each product’s contribution to overall profit.

---

2. **Product Contribution within Category**

```DAX
% of Category Sales :=
DIVIDE(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    CALCULATE(SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]), ALL(Sales[Product]))
)
```

- Within a category, each product shows its share of sales.

---

3. **Top Product by Sales Rank**

```DAX
Product Sales Rank :=
RANKX(
    ALL(Sales[Product]),
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    ,
    DESC
)
```

- Highest-selling product → Rank 1

---

4. **Category Rank by Total Sales**

```DAX
Category Sales Rank :=
RANKX(
    ALL(Sales[Category]),
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    ,
    DESC
)
```

- Electronics usually rank highest, followed by Accessories/Fashion.

---

5. **Ignore Category Filter**

```DAX
Total Sales Ignoring Category :=
CALCULATE(
    SUM(Sales[SalesAmount]),
    ALL(Sales[Category])
)
```

- Returns overall sales total even when a category slicer is applied.
