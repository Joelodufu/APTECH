# **Exercise 3: Dataset Generation with ChatGPT (Using & Extending Sales Dataset)**

### **Objective**

Students will learn how to:

- Use **ChatGPT to generate datasets** when real-world data is not available.
- Extend the existing **Sales dataset** with synthetic data.
- Import ChatGPT-generated data into Power BI for practice.

---

### **Step 1: Generate a New Dataset in ChatGPT**

Students open ChatGPT (or any AI text tool) and use a **prompt like this**:

**Prompt 1: Basic Sales Extension**

> “Generate 30 rows of fictional sales transactions with the following columns: TransactionID, CustomerName, Region (North, South, East, West), ProductCategory (Electronics, Furniture, Clothing), ProductName, Quantity, UnitPrice, TransactionDate (2024), SatisfactionScore (1–5), and SalesRep. Format as a CSV table.”

📌 ChatGPT will output tabular data. Students can copy this into Excel/CSV.

---

### **Step 2: Generate Related Datasets**

To show multi-table Power BI models, ask ChatGPT for **related data**.

**Prompt 2: Customer Table**

> “Generate a list of 20 fictional customers with CustomerID, CustomerName, Age, Gender, Region, and LoyaltyLevel (Gold, Silver, Bronze).”

**Prompt 3: Product Table**

> “Generate a product catalog of 15 products with ProductID, ProductName, ProductCategory, and StandardUnitPrice.”

📌 This allows **relationships**: Sales → Customers → Products.

---

### **Step 3: Clean & Save**

1. Copy ChatGPT output → Paste into **Excel**.
2. Save each dataset as **CSV files**:

   - Sales_Extra.csv
   - Customers.csv
   - Products.csv

3. Load them into **Power BI**.

---

### **Step 4: Combine with Main Dataset**

1. Import **PowerBI_AI_SalesDataset.csv** + the new ChatGPT-generated datasets.
2. Use **Manage Relationships** in Power BI:

   - Link `CustomerName` in Sales to `CustomerName` in Customers table.
   - Link `ProductName` in Sales to `ProductName` in Products table.

3. Now reports can pull **demographics** (Age, Gender) and **product catalog info** alongside sales.

---

### **Step 5: Use ChatGPT for Missing Data Simulation**

Ask ChatGPT to **intentionally create rows with missing values** for practice.

**Prompt 4: Missing Data Simulation**

> “Generate 10 rows of sales transactions where some values for Region, UnitPrice, or SatisfactionScore are missing (blank).”

📌 This will prepare data for **Exercise 5 (Handling Missing Data)**.

---

### **Hands-On Task**

1. Use ChatGPT to generate at least **20 new transactions** and import them into Power BI.
2. Create a **Customers table** with demographics (from ChatGPT) and connect it to Sales.
3. Build a simple **report combining fields**: SalesRep, Region, Age Group, TotalSales.
4. Generate one dataset with **missing values** to prepare for Exercise 5.

---

### **Expected Learning Outcome**

By the end of this exercise, students will:
✅ Know how to **generate synthetic datasets with ChatGPT**.
✅ Extend the main dataset into a **multi-table Power BI model**.
✅ Create **realistic scenarios** for analysis (sales, customers, products).
✅ Prepare data for upcoming topics like **DAX, AI tools, and missing data handling**.
